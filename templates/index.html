How the agent learns

Every three market ticks, it observes net inventory, 5-tick momentum, volatility, order-book imbalance and manual-order pressure. It picks one of seven adjustments to the A–S reservation price, spread or order sizes. Reward = change in account equity − inventory risk penalty. Q-values update at learning rate 0.12 and discount 0.92. Exploration starts at 35% and decays toward 5%. Policy saves every 20 decisions and when stopped. Simulated returns do not guarantee real profits.
